Already a commoner

How much of your life is already a commons?

You share the air, a language and knowledge handed down by people you'll never meet. Commoning is old, and many of us are relearning it.

But it's easy to forget. We start from scratch, good work withers when a project ends, and what we could share stays out of sight.

Tending the commons

Commoning is a practice.

Commoning is something you choose every day. You share what you know, look after what others planted and hold things in trust instead of owning them.

Commoning practice

Tend what you didn't plant

Find something in the commons that someone else contributed, use it, improve it and pass it on. Shared knowledge gets better each time someone picks it up.

Finding our footing

The regenerative movement is growing.

To keep growing, it needs looking after, and we're still learning how to do that together.

We each have a role

The regenerative movement needs shared ground.

A place where your knowledge sits next to everyone else's, less is lost when a project ends, and the quiet work of coordination gets noticed. You don't have to do it all yourself.
