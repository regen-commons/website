Podcast

Conversations that leave something behind

We’re putting together a podcast for Regen Commons. In each episode, two or three people doing regenerative work think through a question that sits between their fields. Afterwards we turn what they said into notes anyone can use.

The pilot is four episodes and it’s still taking shape, so this is a good time to tell us what you’d want from it.

Take part ↓

Working sessions, not interviews

Nobody is being interviewed. Guests come from different organizations and work through a question together.

  1. One question, two or three people

    Each episode takes a question where the guests’ work meets. They bring what they know and what they’re stuck on. We record remotely for 45 to 90 minutes and edit lightly.

  2. Back to the commons

    The host keeps returning to what we hold in common. Every episode ends on the same question: what would you need from a commons like this to bring your work into it?

  3. Something left behind

    Each conversation becomes a summary, glossary terms, relationship map updates and open questions for the Regen Knowledge Commons, the shared knowledge base we’re building. Guests review everything before it’s published.

Four questions for the pilot

This is the current draft. The questions and their order may change as guests help shape them.

  1. 1

    Where the ground is

    Why does it matter to have a knowledge commons that place-based initiatives can adopt, and what are we building?

  2. 2

    The tools beneath

    What does a shared ontology need to hold, what stays free and forkable, and which web3 tools plug in?

  3. 3

    Knowledge on the land

    What do land practitioners need from a commons, and how does practice become shared knowledge without being extracted?

  4. 4

    Back to the ground

    What did the season add up to, what is live now, and what does it take for different groups to share one commons?
