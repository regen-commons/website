Joining the commons

Members are people and organizations who tend the Regen Commons together: sharing knowledge and tools, offering what they can, and taking part in how it’s run.

These questions help the steward council get to know you. There are no trick questions. Plain answers in your own words work best, so please don’t hand the writing to AI.

Your full answers go only to the steward council. If you’re accepted, a short public profile with your name, location and areas of work appears on the map, and other members may receive your contact details.
