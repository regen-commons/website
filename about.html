Who tends the commons

Regen Commons is where the regenerative movement pools knowledge, tools and connections to become more effective together. It is held by its members and tended by a small steward council, under a constitution anyone can read.

The steward council

Five volunteer stewards guide the commons through its foundation period: setting direction, welcoming new members and keeping the work moving. Each of us also works elsewhere in the movement.

How the commons is governed

During this foundation period the Membership Agreement sets out how decisions are made. It draws on the full constitution, which describes where the commons is headed.

Members

People and organizations who tend the commons. Members join community calls, take part in discussions and advise the council on strategy and significant decisions.

Steward council

Guides strategy, admits new members and keeps operations running. Its decisions are documented and shared with members.

Constitution

The commons’ governing agreement: its purpose, values, structure, roles and shared assets.
